Local Courses

Maxwell Park

An open course with a few wooded holes, offering a great variety of both long and short distances.

The layout features multiple pads on some holes and two permanent baskets on every hole, making it suitable for all skill levels.

Course Map UDisc
1850 Gregory St Directions

P.J. Irvin

A "Super 9" course with two tee pads and two baskets on every hole. Uses mature trees and elevation changes to create many unique lines and shot shapes.

Located in a multi-use park with a playground and a port-a-potty available. Walk on the pavement at Te Voert, cross Morris St., and combine with Forrest Park for a full 18!

Course Map UDisc
1601 McKay Dr Directions

Forrest Park

Right across the street from P. J. Irvin, another "Super 9" course with two tee pads and two baskets on every hole.

A great companion to P.J. Irvin. Combine both parks for a full 18-hole experience!

Course Map UDisc
1813 Springfield Rd Directions

Heartland Church Disc Golf Course

Short and technical course located in North Normal. Begins with five open holes and moves into a much more difficult wooded section.

Course Map UDisc
1811 N. Linden St Directions

Flying Iron Disc Golf Course

10-hole course with turf tee pads. Features include an elevated basket, a hazard sand trap, and an island green. Closed while school is in session.

Course Map UDisc
3900 E. Raab Rd Directions

About Us

The BN Disc Golf Club strives to promote the sport of disc golf in the Bloomington Normal area. We work with the City Parks and Rec Departments to develop, maintain, and enhance disc golf courses in our community.

We also run leagues, tournaments, educational programs, and fund raisers for various charities. We encourage more players to get involved with our club and organized disc golf activities that we run.

We uphold high standards for all competitive disc golf events in accordance with PDGA policies and guidelines. We promote and coordinate the efforts of club members and volunteers to broaden the knowledge of disc golf in Central Illinois.

"Most of all, we strive to help people have fun playing the sport we love!"
